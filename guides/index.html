Family and Travel Video Guides

Guides for turning scattered family and travel clips into finished films.

This section is for families and travelers with footage spread across phones, cameras, drones or action cameras, old drives, and cloud folders. Every guide stays practical: gather what matters, shape a watchable story, and finish without pretending the process is instant.

Momentro runs analysis, editing, and export on your computer. Large collections can take real time depending on your device, so a common workflow is: start analysis, let it run, come back to review, make optional adjustments, then start export while you do other things.

Organize footage

Simple systems for scattered footage across multiple devices and shared sources.

  • Create a no-stress intake routine for phones, cameras, drones, old drives, and shared clips
  • Use filenames and folders that still make sense in six months
  • Decide what to archive, what to trim, and what to delete

Make films people rewatch

Story-first methods for short, emotional videos people replay.

  • Pick scenes that build feeling, not just chronology
  • Find a natural beginning, middle, and ending from daily or trip moments
  • Keep pacing tight and leave time for one real review before export

Family trips and events

Repeatable playbooks for birthdays, vacations, milestones, and group travel highlights.

  • Capture key shots without living behind your phone
  • Handle multi-day events and many contributors without getting overwhelmed
  • Turn dozens of mini clips into one coherent highlight film

Preserve and share

Keep memories safe and easy to revisit across years and generations.

  • Build backup habits that work for real family schedules
  • Share with relatives using the right file format for your context
  • Create yearly collections your kids can rewatch later
