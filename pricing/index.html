Pricing FAQ

Is the first film really free?
Yes. Your first full export is free and includes a small watermark. No card. Re-exports of that same free project stay watermarked until you upgrade.
Can I pay on this website?
No — for your purchase to unlock the right project (or your annual plan), checkout runs from the desktop app. This page is only to explain prices.
What does PRO cost?
€39 per year for unlimited watermark-free exports while your subscription is active. Or €9.99 once to remove the watermark for the current project only.
Can I cancel?
Anytime. You keep access until the end of the billing period.
Are my files uploaded to the cloud?
No. Momentro processes your videos locally on your computer. Nothing leaves your device.
